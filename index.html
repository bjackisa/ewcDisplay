Two windows

The controls and the projection are now separate. Open the control room on the operator's screen, and take the display to the church screen.

  1. Open the control room Review scripture, lyrics, the clock and the timer here, then press Launch.
  2. Open the display Drag this window to the projector or extended screen and press F11 for fullscreen.

The control room's Open display window button does the second step for you. Once both are open, everything you set in the console drives the display live — the congregation only ever sees the result.
